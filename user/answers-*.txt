Question 1: Which function called syscall?
Answer: usertrap() called syscall
(From your backtrace: #1 0x000000000001aa6 in usertrap () at kernel/trap.c:68)

Question 2: What is p->trapframe->a7 and what does it represent?
Answer: 0xf (15 in decimal)
This represents the system call number. System call number 15 corresponds to SYS_link in xv6.

Question 3: What was the previous mode that the CPU was in?
Answer: Supervisor mode
(From sstatus = 0x200000022 - bit 8 (SPP) is 1, indicating previous mode was supervisor)
